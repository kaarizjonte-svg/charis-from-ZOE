About This Project

Global Challenges & Solutions is a creative platform that identifies pressing world problems — from climate change to inequality — and uses graphic design, video, and storytelling to visualize solutions and inspire action.

Founded with the belief that design can change the world, we partner with organizations, educators, and communities to make complex global issues accessible and actionable.
